3 · The Anatomy of a Great Prompt

A prompt is simply the instruction you give to an AI. The clearer your instruction, the better the answer. Strong prompts usually have four parts…

Highlighted by AI: “Role, Task, Context and Format together make an AI understand exactly what you want.”

For example, instead of “write about water”, a pro prompt says: “Act as a Class 9 science teacher (role). Explain the water cycle (task) for a student revising for exams (context) in 5 bullet points (format).”
